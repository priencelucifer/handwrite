Handwriting

Spacing is an average: every gap still varies a little like real handwriting, more with higher line wobble.

Page

“Page 1”, “Page 2”… at the top right

Bambu's own start and finish tunes, plus a tune at each pause

Slide the paper back mid-page to write the part the pen can't reach. Off: the text continues on the next page.

Drag the paper on the build plate to where it will sit. Scroll to zoom.

Printing

The pen stays 8 mm above the paper: test a run without ink

Pen ran out, or the run stopped? Leave the paper where it is and save this page again from the line it stopped on. To change or rewrite one word, click it on the page.

First time on this printer? With no pen and no paper, the head visits the far corners of this page's writing at pen-up height and waits at each, so you can see the pen holder clears the bed clips, wiper and frame.
